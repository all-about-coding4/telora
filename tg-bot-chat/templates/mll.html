iPhone · iOS

Install LynkUp on your iPhone using Safari. No App Store required.

How to install on iPhone:
  1. Open Safari click the (at the bottom right)
  2. Tap the Share icon (bottom of the screen)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" (top right)
  5. The LynkUp app will be added on your home screen

Works only in Safari or Chrome other browsers won’t show this option.
