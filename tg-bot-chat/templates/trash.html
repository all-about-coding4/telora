What is Telora?

Telora is a private messaging app built for people who value their digital freedom. Chat with friends, share images, and stay connected — all with end-to-end encryption and zero data mining.

Your privacy matters

  • No phone number or email required
  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted
  • We never store your chat history on our servers
  • No tracking, no analytics, no third-party shares

User responsibility

Telora is provided "as is" and "at your own risk". While we take privacy seriously, you are solely responsible for the security of your device, your encryption keys, and the content you share. Always verify the identity of your chat partners and use strong, unique credentials.

By continuing, you agree to use Telora at your own discretion.
